# MoonTown pack uninstall

Removing the installed MoonTown pack may remove its executable adapter and
static schemas. It must preserve user-owned town state, civic handoffs,
MoonClaw run evidence, generated book artifacts, reviews and accepted
MoonBook knowledge.

Reinstallation or upgrade must not reinterpret an existing
`moontown.civic-synthesis-result.v1` under a different pack version.
