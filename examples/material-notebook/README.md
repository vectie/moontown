# Fictional local-material notebook

`water.md` and `mobility.md` contain independent field observations, not validated
findings. `notebook.json` is the actual edited notebook used in the native test.
It selects two exact passages and keeps the other two imported pages excluded.
The connection between dry soil and an unshaded path is the operator's tentative
question; the path may not describe the plot.

To reproduce with your own existing registered Books, place equivalent fixture
files at `raw/observations.md` in the `water` and `mobility` Book workspaces and
`unselected.md` at `raw/unselected.md` in each, or adapt `selection.json` to your
own IDs and paths. Do not overwrite existing sources. Re-import your files to
obtain their actual digests, then add your own notes. The example's digests are
accepted only if the selected current bytes really match.

The [guide](../../docs/MATERIAL_NOTEBOOK.md) covers import, literal search, exact
source reading, editing, save/reopen, preparation and export. No Book is created,
source overwritten, participant executed or knowledge accepted by this example.
